01 / About me

Progress is built,
not given.

“Champions are not born; they are built through hard work, discipline, perseverance and the courage to learn from every setback.”

Sport has been an important part of my life for as long as I can remember. Moving through different disciplines has taught me consistency, resilience, focus and the value of long-term effort.

Competition has shaped my approach to challenges. I see setbacks as opportunities to understand what needs to improve, return with greater preparation and keep moving towards ambitious goals.

Alongside sport, I am interested in Economics and Psychology, particularly the way financial systems and human decisions influence people's lives. I also want to help create opportunities for children who lack access to sporting equipment, guidance or resources.

My aim is to keep developing as a student, athlete and socially responsible leader.

04 / Social impact

Trustfinity Foundation

Making sporting opportunities more accessible to children.

Registered in January 2025, Trustfinity Foundation grew from a belief that a lack of equipment or resources should not prevent a child from discovering their sporting potential. The initiative collects usable sports equipment and gives it a new purpose by helping connect it with children and participating schools.

489+Sports items collected and distributed
800+Students with potential access to equipment at participating schools

As a competitive athlete, I understand how important suitable equipment and guidance can be. Through Trustfinity Foundation, I hope to contribute to a sporting environment where more children have the chance to participate, learn and develop.
